About

William Alan

Writing is the gateway to the mind and soul.

Biography

G'day, my name's Will, a student of creative writing and computer science. I'm from Brisbane, Australia, and have always loved all things born from creativity and human experience. As of right now, I'm focused primarily on my studies whilst writing bits and pieces in between. I've made a few short stories and pieces of professional writing for university and am looking forward to having the time to create a longer piece after my graduation.

I enjoy writing in many genres, though I particularly enjoy inventing theories on the idea of the human soul and existence in urban fantasy settings, with a little bit of light-hearted comedy along the way. I also thoroughly enjoy writing in a professional manner, such as brochures, instructions manuals, and other pieces of professional writing with which I can take a creative approach.

Professional & Creative Statement

In my eyes, creativity is a skill that can be developed in any way you see fit. It's what makes us so special, our ability to create. I personally enjoy exploring avenues into the unknown with my creativity. Life after death, the human soul, the truth behind our existence and the universe as a whole. I'm enchanted by the idea that no one really knows the truth to any of these concepts, which leaves me with an enormous space to play around with and create in, whilst keeping my work grounded and real.
